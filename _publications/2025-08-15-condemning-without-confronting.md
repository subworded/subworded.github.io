excerpt: 'In this Master’s thesis at UNSW, I conducted a deductive content analysis of 111 news stories from major Australian outlets (The Australian, AFR, SMH, ABC News, Guardian Australia) to examine how foreign leadership is framed in the context of bilateral strategic interests. By testing the indexing hypothesis, my research explored whether Australian media provided independent scrutiny of Indonesia's "democratic backsliding" or if coverage remained aligned with domestic elite perspectives. The findings offer critical insights into media-state relations and the complexities of cross-border political journalism in the Indo-Pacific region.'
