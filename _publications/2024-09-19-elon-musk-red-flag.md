'The ban of X (formerly Twitter) in Brazil serves as a stark warning for Indonesian digital activists who heavily rely on the platform as a digital town hall for socio-political mobilization. To protect the future of the country's progressive movements, activists must urgently build decentralized communication networks rather than depending on a single platform vulnerable to government crackdowns or corporate whims.'
